# Benchmark design

## Goal
Provide a usable method and workflow for developing, testing and evaluating optimizations, with an explicit definition of what each result establishes.

## Authorized
Prepare the discussion and identify missing capabilities. Agree measurement and acceptance decisions with Philip before implementing the design or running new experiments. Routine development-direction repairs remain authorized within the assigned development scope.

## Done when
The agreed design and required tools support an end-to-end evaluation on the chosen machine, including controls, measurement validity, rendering correctness, restoration and understandable results. An accepted optimization is not required to complete this method.

**Shared contract for every measurement role**

- Identity: source commit plus dirty bytes, built/loaded artifacts, original and derived packs, actual native option maps, resource packs, runtime/driver/hardware, resolution/AA, display mode, cap/VSync, heap and instrumentation mode. Baseline is the same original/native workload with candidate disabled; distinguish this from ShaderLOD-absent overhead control. Incumbent is the previously retained configuration, if any, and is an additional comparison, not a replacement for the original reference.
- Workload: world snapshot/ancestry and previous exposure, dimension, route/pose, game time/weather, entities, simulation distances, DH/PBR and effect state, shader temporal history and cache preparation. Record actual observations, not just requests. Development scenes and independent confirmation scenes have different roles; related copies are not independent coverage. Declare progression clock, end condition and actual pose/tick/route checkpoints. Separate fixed-state work comparisons from real-time moving-play comparisons: a route advanced per rendered frame can change traversal speed, while equal wall-time traversal can produce different rendered histories. Quality comparisons explicitly align simulation time, pose or rendered history as appropriate and disclose remaining mismatch; never slow a route to accommodate capture capacity.
- Activation and output: linked artifact, real submitted frames, expected feature execution/opportunity counters, no fallback, unchanged native settings. DH presence/configuration does not prove visible DH work. Missing, blocked, unsupported, inactive and failed remain distinct. Distinguish implementation-enforceable eligibility from favorable scene characteristics merely sampled. Conditions that cannot be enforced must receive adverse-case testing within the actual activation envelope; they cannot silently justify enabling the feature elsewhere.
- Validity and accounting: expected/completed frames or route duration, capture gaps/lost/pending GPU queries, exclusions and reason, all attempts/retries/timeouts, restoration and immutable receipts. No silently truncated rolling buffer or slower-frame dropout. Preserve timestamps, frame/generation IDs, exact measured windows and compact data sufficient to recompute summaries. Predeclare sampling and missingness handling. Partial GPU diagnostics do not invalidate an independently complete frame-production comparison, but cannot represent all-frame GPU behavior.
- Machine conditions: sampled focus/minimization, power policy, thermal/clock observations where available, memory pressure and competing work. Missing telemetry stays missing. Observe drift; do not explain it away by speculative thermal causes or discard unfavorable runs post hoc.

**Diagnose: locate worthwhile work and test the mechanism**

1. Low-instrumentation whole-frame pacing reference and clearly bounded CPU wall/GPU elapsed scopes; never sum overlapping CPU/GPU intervals or rename a callback duration full-frame time. Presentation/display pacing is separately observed where supported; otherwise limit the claim to application frame production.
2. Selected pass timings and actual work counters tied to the hypothesis: calls, draws, samples, pixels, copies/bytes, cache hits AND misses/opportunity denominator. Include instrumentation coverage and unknown remainder. GPU timestamps measure scoped elapsed work, not exclusive hardware occupancy. Qualify supported timer counters, usable resolution and placement sensitivity before attributing pass costs, particularly on tile-based GPUs; nanosecond units alone do not establish sensitivity. Do not force synchronization into acceptance windows to make attribution easier.
3. CPU stacks, thread CPU/waits, allocations/GC, loading/compilation, memory use or DH queue/upload/residency observations only when the suspected mechanism calls for them. Distinguish simulated work from useful completed rendering.
4. Candidate-active, inactive and identity controls; a bounded known-work perturbation where needed to test instrument sensitivity. This is diagnostic evidence, not acceptance or permission to reduce native quality.
5. A real workload probe early, so a tiny isolated win cannot drive extended development before whole-application costs are observed.

**Screen: decide whether to continue, revise or stop**

1. Same-input native-versus-candidate comparisons with balanced/randomized order; record setup, reload and warmup separately. Predeclare arm-symmetric warmup/admission rules. Retain time-to-readiness and non-settling frequency so warmup cannot hide candidate harm. Separate conditioned steady-state from transition-inclusive outcomes; failure to settle prevents the former estimate but remains an outcome. Do not wait for a favorable interval.
2. Timestamped frame production intervals: throughput computed from count/time, distribution including p50/p95 and p99 only with adequate data, slow-frame frequency/duration under predeclared thresholds, and time-order drift. Do not substitute mean instantaneous FPS or inverse GPU time.
3. Matched GPU elapsed samples and relevant CPU cost when explanatory; low-overhead outcome capture is the default. Keep images/readback/detailed traces out of speed windows unless that overhead is itself under test. The minimally instrumented whole-frame comparison is authoritative for performance. Qualify both arms with/without detailed instrumentation when changes to call frequency or scheduling could bias the difference. Preserve the requested cap/VSync mode; any uncapped headroom diagnostic is a separate comparison, not a replacement for capped behavior.
4. A compact candidate-relevant workload set with opportunity-rich and opportunity-poor cases, still AND motion sanity, explicit failure/regression cases. Already-exposed worlds are usable for iteration, not independent confirmation.
5. Unchanged-versus-unchanged controls and instrumentation-on/off comparisons qualify sensitivity. Estimate changes and uncertainty at independent process/session/block level; frame count is not replication. Repetition counts, worthwhile effect and stopping budgets follow pilots and user choices, not a universal fixed number.
6. Report promising, regression, inconclusive, invalid, unsupported and blocked per case; keep failures in reliability counts rather than assigning fake frame times. No retention verdict from the fast screen alone. Screening can remain explicitly exploratory; freeze any stopping/admission rules that determine reported outcomes and label post-hoc discoveries.

**Confirm: justify retention within an agreed scope**

1. Freeze candidate and protocol, then repeat on additional independently chosen scenes/routes and sessions, relevant shader families and machines for the claimed scope. Track tuning exposure and selection across candidates; a fresh copy or seed alone is not guaranteed independence. Do not claim cross-machine support from one host. Before viewing confirmation results, freeze the primary outcome, comparison formula/direction, per-case harm treatment, stopping rule and treatment of candidate selection. Tuning from confirmation consumes those cases as development evidence for the next revision; no mandatory universal multiple-testing correction is assumed.
2. Representative stationary and moving play, streaming/transition behavior where relevant, near/far geometry, effect boundaries and worst cases. DH and PBR require actual exercised rendering; dimension/weather/time transitions are selected by affected paths, not a mandatory Cartesian product.
3. Matched stills, short motion sequences and targeted effect checks under declared scene/temporal alignment; unchanged-control variability, image-difference maps and human review. Preserve color space/HDR/exposure assumptions. Record sequence cadence, gaps, onset/recovery and targeted regions. For temporal changes include disocclusion, camera cuts, history resets and convergence. At harness qualification, a known visual-defect test artifact must be detected by capture and inspection; unchanged controls alone only calibrate false alarms. Defect controls qualify the selected defect classes, not all possible visual failures. A single image score or byte equality is not a universal motion-quality oracle.
4. Whole-frame outcomes, tails and per-case regressions with independent-run uncertainty. Check small subsystem gains survive application overhead. Do not hide regressions in a global average; broad/scoped retention rule remains the user's decision.
5. Off/Cancel/failed Apply, reload/world/dimension changes, unsupported-input refusal and state restoration. Use existing functional tests and add candidate-specific boundary cases. Verify disabled overhead, preparation/reload latency and peak memory where the implementation changes them; compare retained combinations against the incumbent combination and original instead of adding singleton gains. If claiming session-level value, report preparation cost and recurring gain separately with the assumed usage horizon or break-even; the user chooses the relevant horizon.
6. A bounded sustained representative run checks drift, memory growth, cache evolution and accumulating work. For caching, deferred work or streaming, observe useful completion, backlog and recovery so moving required work beyond the window cannot masquerade as a gain. Its duration follows the suspected lifecycle and budget; no universal endurance promise. Exercise interruption, recovery, incomplete-output publication and cleanup at harness qualification, not necessarily on every candidate run.

**Sufficiency and deliberate exclusions**

The inventory covers identity, actual work, user-facing outcome, correctness, uncertainty, coverage, operational reliability and reproducibility. Collect a field only if it can change a development decision or validate another field. Lightweight outcome/validity facts are common; detailed traces and broader workloads are conditional. No all-passes/all-counters trace on every run, all-pack/all-setting/all-machine Cartesian suite, permanent full video, byte-identical dynamic frames, universal p-value/repeat count, external power apparatus or input-to-photon rig by default. These become necessary if the claim expands to their corresponding mechanism, deployment coverage, energy or interaction latency. Missing presentation telemetry precludes a display-smoothness claim; it does not turn a CPU callback into such evidence. Sufficiency is conditional: each identified material failure mode for the chosen decision must have a check or an explicit claim limitation. New mechanisms or claims reopen only affected requirements, not automatically every collection mode.

**Research and repository implications**

- [Khronos timer-query specification](https://registry.khronos.org/OpenGL/extensions/ARB/ARB_timer_query.txt): CPU timing does not establish completion of GPU commands; asynchronous queries avoid forced completion. Design implication: preserve query completion/coverage and keep timing domains separate.
- [PresentMon documentation](https://github.com/GameTechDev/PresentMon): application, GPU and display observations differ, and OpenGL/platform support has limitations. It is a possible machine-specific cross-check, not an adopted dependency or a promise for Asahi.
- [Kalibera and Jones](https://kar.kent.ac.uk/33611/): estimate variance at relevant repetition levels and spend runs where uncertainty arises. Design implication: pilot-driven independent repetitions and effect uncertainty.
- [VM warmup study](https://arxiv.org/abs/1602.00602): do not assume a fixed warmup guarantees stable VM behavior. Record startup and drift; define bounded admission rules.
- [NVIDIA FLIP](https://github.com/NVlabs/flip): rendered-image comparisons can guide localized inspection; temporal behavior still needs sequences and matched history. No universal perceptual threshold is adopted.
- [JDK 21 Flight Recorder guidance](https://docs.oracle.com/en/java/javase/21/troubleshoot/troubleshoot-performance-issues-using-jfr.html): diagnostic modes can affect the workload, especially heap-statistics collection. Measure observer cost rather than borrowing a generic overhead claim.
- Current DevelopmentProfiler measures callback wall duration; BoundedProfiler retains 4096 samples and GPU collector 64 completed pairs with rolling loss. These are diagnostics, not complete benchmark windows. ProfileExport is manual; no maintained comparison/scene replay/visual oracle is implemented. Existing process/receipt/functional machinery can be reused but needs its own benchmark lifecycle qualification.
- Historical constraints: unchanged Asahi comparisons drifted; repeated candidates weakened initial gains; GPU captures had incomplete coverage; motion capture backpressure broke timing interpretation; old getter errors invalidated sampler claims. Read linked results before selecting mechanisms.

Agreed proportional limits: GPU counters remain optional when unavailable and not needed for a scoped whole-frame claim; observer qualification is renewed when instrumentation/interaction changes, not every unchanged run; diagnostic perturbations and visual controls qualify specific sensitivity, not arbitrary bugs; equal rendered-frame count is not required for a real-time playback claim. None of these limits may hide missing evidence for the claimed outcome.
