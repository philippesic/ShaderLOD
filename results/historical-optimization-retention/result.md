# None of the sixteen historical candidates met the original keep bar

The earlier screen does not supply an optimization to enable by default. It does supply scoped results and rejected approaches worth consulting before repeating the work. Their implementations remain in main's preserved Git history, not an old working-checkout dependency.

## Conditions and limitations

Scope: Original repository's final decision. Evidence level: source-inspected. No new measurements. This is a historical evidence import; no new experiment was executed.

## Decision

Discard. The sixteen candidates remain excluded from active implementation under the historical retention decision; scoped benefits are not claims of general qualification.

2026-11-12 correction: "discard" overstated the original record. Philip's 2026-11-08 decision reads: retain an optimization only if it showed a positive result across all four supported shaders and both completed machines; none qualified; all sixteen are parked. A held or unavailable cell did not count as positive. The RTX 5080 conclusion in the same archive kept three scoped gains as experimental: G01 on MakeUp, G02 on BSL and C02 on three packs ([F11, F12, F22](../../FINDINGS.md)). The rule also cannot be met by a mechanism that needs hardware one machine lacks, or by one that is bound to exact pack sources and was implemented for one pack. Whether that rule still governs is Philip's decision; the [current direction](../../docs/optimization-philosophy.md) describes per-pack eligibility with refusal elsewhere. Reason for the correction: the import flattened "parked with scoped gains" into "discarded".

Two limits apply to every cell of that screen. Asahi cells were mostly single runs inside the −9.7% to +10.2% no-change spread of that setup ([F2](../../FINDINGS.md)), so a single Asahi run under ten percent establishes neither a loss nor a gain. The RTX 5080 cells were precise (unchanged-shader controls within 0.25%), but the widespread losses on three packs were never explained ([F37](../../FINDINGS.md)).
