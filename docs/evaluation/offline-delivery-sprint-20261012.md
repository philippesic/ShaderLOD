is an isolated implementation under the explicit request, not a reopened speed
trial or a claim of novelty for weight carry.

stores nextWeight beside native lastDistance only after accumulation. All early
exits, counts, positions, shadow/texture work, subtraction, feedback and final
transforms remain. Off/Quality/Performance/Aggressive/invalid controls and
compile-excluded paths retain native weight evaluation. For m visits to the
weight site, active lazy evaluation goes2m→m+1 when m>0, and zero stays zero;
an entity-rejected final visit is counted even though it does not accumulate.

These models are not GLSL driver bit equivalence or a speed measurement.
