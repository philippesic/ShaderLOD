# The Mac backlog closed with mixed outcomes and no new generated-pack winner

The final fifteen-item consolidation recorded active losing depth paths, mostly negative or inconclusive half-AO, and limited generated-control-relative shadow/trace observations. Large repeated source-validation costs were demonstrated in render paths; repairing them did not prove the shader reductions profitable. Sampler reuse remained a cache-hit observation, spatial producer coverage was insufficient, and compile-presence and measurement prototypes retained explicit gaps.

## Conditions and limitations

Evidence level: source-inspected historical import. M3 Max Mac, NeoForge 1.21.1, Iris 1.8.12, Sodium 0.6.13, native settings and AA; current graph screens used 1920×1200 coast views. Earlier scenes and builds remain separate. Native means original shader/core Off with ShaderLOD still loaded, not a mod-absent baseline. No new run was performed.

## Decision

Retain the repair and negative evidence. Do not repeat unchanged losing scene profiles or treat control-relative gains as native-relative gains. Unresolved quality, compatibility and hardware coverage remain separate.
