The [index](retirement-supplement/index.json) maps original paths and SHA-256 identities
to 259 distinct reports, 1,478 structured observations, 987 quantitative ledger
excerpts and 431 authored source variants. Repeated structured values are stored
once; referenced internal values are resolved by the verifier. The scan inspected
66,598 textual source/archive entries and compared them against existing compact
records and published source. These are records, not independent experiments.

Evidence level: source-inspected for the older experimental accounts; cpu-integration
for extraction and integrity verification. Earlier macOS, Asahi and Windows tuples
remain separate. Long sample arrays are explicitly reduced to counts and numerical
summaries; repeated state dumps, operational diaries and replay environments are
superseded. Reports preserve authors' observations and limits, not a new independent
scientific replication. Full raw images, temporal traces and exact historic runtime
replay are not promised. Failed, blocked and inconclusive records are retained too.

Decision: retain this compact supplement and the existing scoped findings; discard
superseded archive bulk after verification. Keep retired source checkouts explicitly
marked ready for deletion, with original refs in local Git recovery. Current work
has no dependency on either checkout. This supplements the earlier extraction;
its original inventory did not account for these separate home-directory copies.
