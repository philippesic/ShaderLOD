## Measurement and optimization failures

| Issue | Evidence | Root cause | Addressed by |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Optimizing before measuring | Campaigns G01–G10, C01–C02, N01–N04: 16 candidates built and screened before any frame-time profile existed. The cost map started 8 November 2026. 0 of 16 met the retention rule; all parked. Best near-miss cited in the problem map: MakeUp G01 +8.14% Asahi, +3.24% RTX, MakeUp only. BSL G02 +3.95% on BSL but −5.67% and −10.01% on Complementary; C02 bloom helps NVIDIA only. | The question "where does frame time go?" was asked last. | Remaining-stages source order ports profiler contracts after identity and lifecycle, and admits no parked candidate without a new go. Unaddressed until a later authorized candidate study. |
| Benchmark noise larger than the effects | Six unchanged-shader Bliss/native463 closed-lid blocks: null effects −9.70% to +10.24%; process means 76.65 to 100.69 ms (+31.36%); approximate process-level 95% interval [−6.22%, +8.70%]; four failed stability gates. A ~3% gain cannot be told from that noise. This is a specific failed calibration, not a universal ±10% error bar. | No do-nothing calibration gate; no clock, thermal, or presentation identity on every result. | Plan section 9A and stage 4a analyzer: 6 to 24 null blocks proposed, process/block effects as the unit, no `--force`, no restart-until-positive. Not built until the benchmark stage. |
| Sparse GPU timing was read as a full breakdown, including zeros | Inspected MakeUp coverage capture: 163 frame records, 162 complete intervals, GPU results on 39 frames (24.07%), 43 unresolved queries, one imbalanced scope. Cadence 4 sparsifies samples by design, so the fraction alone is not query failure, but a whole-frame all-pass total cannot be inferred. Legacy and HUD paths could treat missing as 0 ms. | Missing was not a first-class outcome. | Contract types: measured or missing with a reason; measured zero needs count 1; UNKNOWN or missing-as-zero fails qualification. Stage 0 has the contracts; live thresholds wait for stage 4. |
| Health was UNKNOWN and required DH paths were unexercised | The same capture's health was UNKNOWN; several counters were null, including `unresolvedTail=43` and `imbalancedScopes=1`. Conversion, shadow, and the later depth handoff were CPU-tested or unexercised, not live-qualified. | Completion reports mixed live and CPU work. | Evidence levels in `docs/evidence-levels.md`; unknown is FAIL for qualification; `./verify --runtime` exits 2 until stage 4. FINDINGS already seeds these rows. |
| Complementary DH originals failed to link | Run-05 Reimagined and run-06 Unbound failed enabled-DH composite1 linking with "Too many fragment shader texture samplers" on this Asahi runtime. | Hardware and pack limit, then a risk of lowering settings to force a pass. | Keep the failures as negative fixtures. Do not relax samplers. Future qualification must reproduce or explicitly resolve them on a new identity. |
| Platform rules were copied in five places | RTX audit: 217 operations, 30 invalid. Runs used the wrong OpenGL version. One mismatch touched 63 shared adapter clauses. | Capability knowledge was duplicated instead of declared once. | Planned one capability definition driving admission and the campaign plan; pack specs discovered as files. Lands in the source and benchmark stages. |
