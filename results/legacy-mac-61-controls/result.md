# The legacy Mac program contained 61 controls, not 61 proven performance wins

The MacBook Pro-era program is separate from the later sixteen-candidate screen. It defined 61 canonical controls across nine shader families, with 549 source/applicability dispositions. The October 17 handoff established implementation and recovery evidence within its stated scope; it did not qualify all controls for rendering quality or speed.

[Browse the 61 controls](catalog.md). [All 549 preserved family/control records](result.json).

## Conditions and limitations

Evidence level: source-inspected historical import. The nine families are Photon, Solas, AstraLex, Reimagined, Unbound, Bliss, MakeUp, Sildur and BSL. The handoff covered Minecraft 1.21.1/NeoForge; independent-control, source, runtime, quality and performance evidence are distinct. The later October 22 Mac impact report completed fifteen backlog dispositions, not all 61 performance qualifications. Some old Mac raw artifacts are not present in the local archive; their absence prevents a complete rerun claim.

## Decision

Retain this catalog and its scoped findings for future development. Do not apply the later sixteen-candidate discard decision to these 61 controls as a blanket result. Do not import implementations or adopt settings automatically. Revisit a mechanism using its own source, activation, correctness and performance evidence.
