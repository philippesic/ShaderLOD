# Optimization direction

ShaderLOD is one plug-and-play product. Seek mechanisms that help multiple shader packs and devices, using small explicit integrations where their render graphs differ. Start development with one pinned pack to make causes understandable. A win there is a development result; it does not establish broad support.

Prefer removing redundant work, improving data flow and reuse, or making mathematically equivalent changes. An internal approximation can also be useful when it preserves perceptual quality in the actual activation envelope. Original archives, user-selected options, native output resolution and AA remain intact. Do not quietly lower those controls to win a benchmark. Record the approximation and inspect the cases most likely to reveal it, including motion and transitions.

A good module separates its mechanism from its pack binding. Eligibility identifies exact source/graph/options and required hardware/runtime capabilities. A missing or unrecognized condition produces a safe refusal or tested fallback. Do not activate globally because a selected sunny still happens to look good. Conditions the implementation cannot enforce remain part of the test obligation.

Use the short development comparison to learn whether an idea deserves work. Use profiling to explain where time goes; labels are not causal proof. Before retaining a contribution, freeze its candidate and comparison policy, qualify useful benefit and bounded harm, inspect quality, and exercise the actual enabled combination. Original, incumbent and candidate have different meanings. Never add singleton percentage gains to predict the shipped set.

Broadness is earned by coverage. A structurally different pack with the optimization actually active tests reuse; a fallback pack tests refusal, not speedup. Cross-device and cross-runtime claims need their own evidence. The current product controller admits the pinned development pack; extending product admission requires explicit provider eligibility and focused live qualification. Generic benchmark selection/profiling can inspect other original packs already. Adding a benchmark adapter does not broaden product eligibility.

Do not optimize for route IDs, capture mode or known benchmark output. Benchmark-only delay and visual-fault providers are explicit dev controls and stay out of the shipping JAR. Negative and inconclusive results are useful knowledge. Preserve their evidence, remove discarded implementation when authorized, and keep FINDINGS brief.
